Our Heritage of Faith

Nestled along the serene coastline of Trivandrum, St. Thomas Church, Poonthura, stands as a majestic testament to centuries of unwavering faith. As a prominent parish under the Kovalam Forane, it has served as a spiritual beacon for the coastal community for generations.

The parish is not just a structure of stone, but a living legacy of the Apostle St. Thomas. From its historical roots to its current role in the Latin Archdiocese, Poonthura Church remains the heart of local devotion and cultural identity.
