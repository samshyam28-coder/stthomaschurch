Lineage of Parish Priests

Since our founding, St. Thomas Church, Poonthura, has been blessed with the dedicated leadership of many visionary shepherds.
This lineage serves as a testament to our enduring faith and the continuous guidance of the Latin Archdiocese of Trivandrum.
We honor the sacrifices, wisdom, and pastoral care of every Parish Priest
who has nurtured our community’s spiritual growth throughout the generations.
